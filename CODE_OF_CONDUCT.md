# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community and the people affected by the product.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing another person’s private information, introducing real patient data, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the [`eangelica2014`](https://github.com/eangelica2014) profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
