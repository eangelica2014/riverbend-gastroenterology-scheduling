## Audit Standard

This audit separates **assignment compliance**, **evidence of role fit**, and **production boundaries**. A requirement is marked **Verified** only when the requested artifact exists and its behavior or presentation has been inspected or tested. **Exceeded** means the submission adds useful evidence beyond the stated ask without substituting novelty for the requirement. **Recording-ready** means the optional video content, deck, interactions, and timed script exist, but the candidate still performs the final recording.

> **Audit principle:** Never ask the hiring manager to accept a claim when the repository, prototype, scenario lab, document, or CI run can show the evidence directly.
