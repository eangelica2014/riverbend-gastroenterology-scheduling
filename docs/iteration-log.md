## Validation Updates

Validation initially ran on **August 26, 2026** against policy version `1.0.0` and reference date `2026-08-26`. A later hiring-manager audit found that location FAQ behavior existed but did not have its own named regression scenario. The catalog and suite were expanded rather than allowing a broad FAQ claim to hide that gap. The final suite passes **19 of 19 assertions**, including a direct executable location response test, and replays **25 of 25 catalog scenarios**. Coverage includes age and three-year boundaries, minor/inactive-coverage/missing-policy/discharge denials, provider continuity, Dr. Crane’s Thursday behavior, earliest-slot ordering, hours/location/parking knowledge, emergency and urgent-clinical preemption, duplicate-name privacy, DOB mismatch privacy, explicit confirmation, and cancellation isolation.

TypeScript completed with no errors. The production build completed successfully. Vite reported one non-blocking optimization warning because the main JavaScript chunk is larger than 500 kB; a production follow-up would introduce route-level dynamic imports, but the take-home keeps the routes in one inspectable artifact for evaluator reliability.

The six representative routes—case brief, agent, architecture, scenario lab, platform, and experiments—were captured at a 1440 × 900 viewport for visual review. That review confirmed the Clinical Field Notebook direction and identified a real defect: three first-generation editorial assets rendered failure placeholders. The prototype was changed rather than rationalized: simpler Riverbend illustrations were generated, the URLs were replaced, the logo lockup became visible in every major page header, and semantic evidence accents were strengthened. The live experience retains a fixed folio navigation rail; full-page capture behavior intentionally removes non-top fixed chrome.

Accessibility decisions were exercised in implementation: all voice actions retain a text path, scripted scenarios require no microphone, a skip link precedes navigation, controls expose labels and pressed/expanded states, focus treatment is visible, and nonessential motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Browser speech remains an optional progressive enhancement and is not presented as production telephony.
