## Empathy-to-Design Demonstration

This section makes empathy observable in the product rather than describing it as a personality trait.

| Method move  | Human concern observed                                                                                 | Voice-agent design consequence                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Observe**  | A caller may be anxious, multitasking, hard of hearing, or unsure which administrative detail matters. | Ask one question at a time, keep text available, and display interim speech so a misheard phrase can be noticed before it becomes a decision.                             |
| **Frame**    | Repeating a story or hearing private details before verification can feel exposing and erode trust.    | Carry the caller’s intent forward, use phone only to find candidates, verify DOB before protected appointment detail, and explain the next safe step without blame.       |
| **Build**    | A caller needs orientation, not an opaque spinner; a handoff should not feel like failure.             | Show a plain-language progress rail—intent, identity, coverage, policy, availability, confirmation, outcome—and present front-desk or nurse transfer as a supported path. |
| **Validate** | A polite response can still disclose, search, or write incorrectly.                                    | Test forbidden reads and tools, emergency preemption, exact confirmation, state mutation, and transfer destination—not sentiment alone.                                   |
| **Improve**  | A misunderstood utterance or repeated transfer is evidence about the system.                           | Preserve a safe transcript and decision trace so an FDE can diagnose the right layer and propose a human-approved change.                                                 |

**Exact narration — approximately 45 seconds:**

> “My empathetic problem-solving method changed the interaction order, not just the tone. I observed that a caller may be anxious, distracted, hard of hearing, or unsure which detail matters. I framed the problem as cognitive load plus privacy risk. That led me to ask one question at a time, preserve the caller’s intent so they do not repeat the story, show a real-time transcript so a misheard phrase is visible, and explain progress without exposing patient detail. Phone finds a candidate; date of birth verifies before protected information. If the system cannot safely complete the task, the human path is presented as supported care—not a dead end. I then validate empathy with negative guarantees: no disclosure after a mismatch, no slot search after an eligibility stop, no write without an explicit yes, and no scheduling through urgent language.”

**On-screen action:** Point, in order, to **Live transcript**, **Scheduling progress**, **Text always available**, and the adjacent **Decision trace**. Then start the booking replay. The sequence visually connects human need → design choice → testable contract.
